About Us

S-Clinical is a clinical research consulting company. Founded by experts in the clinical research industry with a combined 35+ years of industry experience. We specialize in providing high quality and cost-effective consulting services to the individuals aspiring career in the clinical research field, clinical research sites, sponsors, and CROs in the following areas:

Clinical Research Training & Placement

We provide training for the individuals aspiring to a career in clinical research. For your convenience these courses are offered on-line. We also provide placement services.

Site Management Services

For the physicians who are interested in expanding their private practice/clinic in to clinical research site, we provide site management support to help prepare your site(s) ready to conduct clinical trials.

Software Solutions

For the Sponsors, CROs, and clinical research sites, we build customized softwares to meet your business needs in order to conduct clinical trials effectively and within the study budget.

Your Support System

The goal of our consulting service is to allow you the chance to concentrate on your business and the services you offer. Essentially, we work behind the scenes and act as your partner in success. Contact us to learn more about our services and capabilities.
